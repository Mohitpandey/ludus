# Camels are fascinating creatures!

They are large, even-toed ungulates (meaning they have hooves and walk on two toes) native to the deserts of Asia and Africa. There are two main types: Dromedary camels (with one hump) and Bactrian camels (with two humps).

Here's a bit more about them:

* **Humps:** Those iconic humps aren't filled with water, but with fat! This fat provides nourishment when food is scarce, allowing camels to survive for long periods in harsh environments.

* **Adaptations:** Camels are incredibly well-adapted to desert life. They can go for days without water, have thick eyelashes and closable nostrils to protect from blowing sand, and wide feet to prevent sinking in the sand.

* **Uses:** For centuries, camels have been used by humans for transportation, milk, meat, and wool. They are sometimes called "ships of the desert" because of their importance in desert travel.

* **Temperament:** While they can be stubborn, camels are generally gentle and intelligent animals.
